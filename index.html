About this map

BOUNDARIES ≠ PERMISSION

This early-access map filters the Fee land records in the USGS Protected Areas Database of the United States (PAD-US) 3.0 for selected federal, state, and local public managers in California. It excludes private records and overlapping designation-only records. The live ArcGIS layer is hosted outside USGS and reproduces this older PAD-US release.

PAD-US is a national inventory assembled from agency and partner data, not a real-time closure or camping-permission feed. California's PAD-US 3.0 update included state, regional, local, and nonprofit data from CPAD; coverage and update dates vary by source. Its boundaries are generalized and may overlap or be incomplete.

Do not use this map to decide whether a specific campsite is legal or open. Contact the managing office and check current restrictions, access, fire rules, and conditions. A missing boundary does not prove land is private or unavailable.
